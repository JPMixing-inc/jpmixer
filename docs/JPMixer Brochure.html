Browser-Based Monitor Mix Control for DiGiCo Consoles
Mac & Windows

Give Every Musician Control of Their Own Mix — From Their Phone

JPMixer connects to your DiGiCo console and lets musicians dial in their own in-ear monitor or wedge mix in real time from any smartphone, tablet, or laptop. No extra hardware. No app to download. Just WiFi and a browser.

For the Engineer

  • 🎚
    Real-Time Fader Control Every aux send level goes directly to the DiGiCo console via OSC with no noticeable lag.
  • 📋
    Monitor Engineer View Full-screen grid of every aux mix side by side — all channels, all faders, fully interactive.
  • 💾
    Ear Scene Recall Save a full mix per DiGiCo snapshot. Mixes push automatically when the snapshot fires — no action needed during the show.
  • 🎛
    Console Controller Full input strip faders, control groups, and aux output masters — controllable from any browser on the network.
  • 📁
    My Mixes & Built-In EQ Performers save personal named mixes anytime, and shape their own tone with an 8-band aux EQ — all from their phone.
  • 📱
    iPad OSC Relay Forwards DiGiCo OSC to iPads running external control apps alongside JPMixer.

System Requirements

DiGiCo SD or Quantum console with OSC / DiGiCo Pad external control enabled

Mac or Windows computer on the same network as the console

Any phone, tablet, or laptop with a browser

For the Musicians

What musicians can do from their phone

  • Adjust any channel in their personal mix in real time
  • Mute or solo individual channels without affecting anyone else
  • Control left/right pan for stereo IEM users
  • Save their mix for each song — recalls automatically during the show
  • Save personal named mixes anytime with My Mixes, and shape their own tone with built-in EQ
  • Add to iPhone home screen — works like a native app, stays connected when screen locks
  • No app download, no account, no setup — just open a link

How It Works

  1. 1
    Install JPMixer on your Mac or Windows computer The app runs quietly in the menu bar or system tray and starts a local web server automatically.
  2. 2
    Enter your DiGiCo console's IP and OSC ports JPMixer connects to the console and reads all channel names, aux counts, and current levels.
  3. 3
    Share the link with your musicians They open it in Safari or Chrome on their phone — no login, no download. Their mix is live instantly.
