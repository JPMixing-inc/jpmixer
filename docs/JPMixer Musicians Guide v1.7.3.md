## 1. Getting Connected

JPMixer works entirely in your web browser — no app to download from any app store. All you need is your phone on the right WiFi network.

1. Connect your phone or tablet to the **stage WiFi network** — ask your monitor engineer for the network name and password
2. Open **Safari** (iPhone/iPad) or **Chrome** (Android)
3. Type the mixer address into the address bar — your engineer will give you this (it looks like `http://192.168.x.x:8080`)
4. The JPMixer page loads — you're in

> **Use Safari on iPhone/iPad.** JPMixer is built for Safari on iOS. Chrome and other browsers will work but won't give you the best experience — some features like adding to your home screen and staying connected while your screen is locked won't work the same way.

> **Can't reach the page?** Make sure your phone is on the stage WiFi, not your mobile data or a different network. The mixer only works on the same local network as the console.

## 2. Installing on Your Home Screen

You can save JPMixer to your phone's home screen so it opens like a real app — full screen, no browser bar, and it remembers your mix. This is the best way to use it, especially on iPhone/iPad.

1. Open the mixer in Safari
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen — it looks like a box with an arrow pointing up
3. Scroll down in the share sheet and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add** in the top right corner
5. The JPMixer icon appears on your home screen — tap it any time to open your mix

### Why add it to your home screen?

When you open JPMixer from your home screen icon it runs full-screen with no browser chrome — just your mix. More importantly, on iOS the connection stays alive even when your phone screen locks or you switch apps. When you come back, it reconnects instantly.

If you open it in Safari as a regular tab, iOS may suspend the connection when you switch away. From the home screen icon it behaves like a native app.

## 3. Selecting Your Mix

When you first open JPMixer you'll see the mix picker — a list of all the monitor mixes on the show. Each mix is one aux bus on the console (your wedge, your IEM, your side-fill, etc.).

Tap your name or your mix name to enter it. Your mix fills the screen and you're ready to go.

### Switching Mixes

Tap the mix bar ("Select Mix") at the top of the screen at any time to go back to the picker and choose a different mix. This is useful if you're covering multiple positions or want to check someone else's mix.

> **Your mix is remembered.** Once you've selected a mix, JPMixer remembers it. Next time you open the app — even after closing it or losing WiFi — it goes straight back to your mix without you needing to tap anything.

## 4. Adjusting Your Levels & Pan

Each channel in your mix has a fader you can drag to set the level. The channel name and icon are shown at the left of each row.

### Moving a Fader

- Drag **up** to turn a channel up, **down** to turn it down
- The dB value updates live as you move it
- Dragging all the way down takes the channel to silence (–∞ dB)
- Tap the dB number next to a fader to type an exact value instead of dragging

### Pan

Tap the **PAN** toggle at the top right to reveal a pan control alongside each fader — drag it left or right to position that channel in your stereo mix. This only matters if you're listening in stereo (stereo IEMs).

| Control | Location | What it does |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Mix bar | Top centre | Shows your current mix. Tap to switch to a different mix. |
| Connection dot | Top right | Green = live connection to the console. |
| PAN toggle | Top right | Reveals pan controls alongside each fader. |
| ☰ menu | Top right | Save Mix, Ear Scenes, and fader size — see Section 9. |
| Snapshot bar | Just under the header | Shows the current song/snapshot name, and your Ear Scene status once a mix is selected — see Section 8. |

> **Your changes go straight to the console.** Every fader move is sent to the DiGiCo console in real time. There is no "apply" button — what you hear is what you set.

## 5. Mute & Solo

Every channel row has two small buttons: **M** and **S**. These let you quickly silence or isolate channels in your mix without moving any faders.

### Mute — M

Tap **M** to mute a channel. The channel goes to silence and the row dims. The button lights red while active.

Tap it again to unmute — the fader snaps back to exactly where it was before you muted. Your levels are never lost.

### Solo — S

Tap **S** to solo a channel. Every other channel in your mix goes silent — only the soloed channel plays. The button lights amber. Tap again to release the solo and bring everything back.

You can solo multiple channels at once — all soloed channels play, everything else is silent.

> **Good for dialling in one instrument.** Solo is useful when you want to set the level of one specific channel without everything else competing. Solo it, set the level, then release.

> **This only affects your mix.** Muting or soloing a channel only affects your own monitor mix. It has no effect on FOH, other musicians' mixes, or the main PA. You can't accidentally mute something for the audience.

## 6. Built-In Aux EQ

If your engineer has turned EQ on for your mix, you can shape your own tone with an 8-band parametric EQ, right from your phone.

### Opening the EQ

1. Make sure your mix is selected
2. Tap ☰ → **Equalizer** (only shown if EQ is enabled for your mix — ask your engineer if you don't see it)
3. A curve with band handles appears

### Adjusting a Band

1. Tap a band handle on the curve to select it — three sliders appear below: **FREQ**, **GAIN**, and **Q**
2. Drag the handle directly on the curve for quick changes, or use the sliders underneath for precise control
3. **GAIN** boosts or cuts that frequency (−18 to +18 dB)
4. **Q** controls how narrow or wide the affected range is
5. Tap **Reset** to flatten the selected band back to 0 dB

EQ changes apply to your mix immediately — there's nothing separate to save. Your EQ settings are automatically included whenever you save a My Mix or an Ear Scene, so they'll come back exactly as you left them.

## 7. My Mixes — Save Your Personal Sound

My Mixes are your own personal, named mixes — completely separate from any song or snapshot. Save one for "my usual IEM balance," another for "loud room," another for "acoustic set" — whatever makes sense for you — and load any of them back at any time, on any mix, even weeks later.

### Saving a Mix

1. Dial in your levels, pan, and EQ exactly how you want them
2. Tap ☰ → **Save Mix** — this opens the **My Mixes** panel
3. Tap **+ Save Current Mix As…**
4. Type a name and tap **Save**

### Loading or Deleting a Mix

Open ☰ → **Save Mix** any time to see everything you've saved. Tap a mix's name to load it onto whichever mix you currently have selected. Tap the 🗑 next to a mix to delete it.

> **My Mixes never disappear.** Unlike Ear Scenes (Section 8), your My Mixes are permanent — they survive closing the app, restarting your phone, and JPMixer itself relaunching. Come back next week and they'll still be there. If you ever clear your phone's browser data, JPMixer keeps a backup on its end and restores them automatically the next time you open the mixer.

## 8. Ear Scenes — Automatic Mix Per Song

An Ear Scene is a saved snapshot of your entire mix — levels, pan, and EQ — tied to one specific song on the console. When your engineer fires that song's snapshot, JPMixer instantly moves your faders to match what you saved. No tapping needed during the show.

> **Ear Scenes start fresh every show.** Unlike My Mixes, Ear Scenes are cleared out every time JPMixer is launched — so nobody accidentally plays last week's mixes over tonight's set. If you want something that's always there no matter when you show up, save it as a **My Mix** (Section 7) instead.

### How to Save an Ear Scene for a Song

1. Make sure the **song name** appears in the bar just under the header — this is the current console snapshot. If it shows "—", wait a moment for it to load
2. Set your mix exactly the way you want it for that song
3. Tap ☰ → **Ear Scenes** → **Save Ear Scene**

A confirmation toast appears, and a banner shows up under the song name: **🔒 Mix Locked for [song name]**.

### Your Faders Lock After Saving

Once you save an Ear Scene for the song that's currently playing, your faders **lock** so they can't be bumped by accident. If you try to move one, you'll see: *"Mix locked — tap the banner above to unlock."*

Tap the banner itself to unlock and make changes. The banner tells you what's going on at all times:

| Banner says... | Meaning |
|-----------------|---------|
| **Not Saved** | You haven't saved an Ear Scene for this song yet |
| **🔒 Mix Locked for [song]** | Saved and locked. Tap to unlock and edit |
| **🔓 Unlocked — Editing** | You can move faders freely right now. Tap to lock again |
| **↺ Changed Since Save — Tap to Revert** | You've moved something since your last save. Tap to snap back to your saved mix |

To remove a saved Ear Scene entirely, tap ☰ → **Ear Scenes** → **Delete Ear Scene** (this replaces "Save Ear Scene" once one exists for the current song).

### Fixing a Different Song's Mix Anytime

You're not limited to editing whatever song is live right now. Tap ☰ → **Ear Scenes** → **Browse Ear Scenes** to see every song you've saved a mix for. The one currently playing is marked **LIVE** — tapping any *other* song opens an editor where you can adjust that saved mix's faders without touching what's actually coming out of your ears right now. Nothing you change there goes anywhere until you tap **Save**.

This means if you realize mid-show that tomorrow's opener needs a fix, you can go fix it right then — you don't have to wait for the engineer to fire that snapshot.

### During the Show

When the engineer fires a snapshot, JPMixer automatically pushes your saved mix to the console and your faders move to the saved positions. You don't need to do anything — just play.

> **Check with your engineer first.** Auto-recall needs to be turned on by the engineer in JPMixer's settings. If your mixes aren't recalling automatically during the show, let your monitor engineer know and they can enable it.

> **Save during soundcheck.** The best time to save your Ear Scenes is during soundcheck. Go through each song's snapshot with the engineer, dial in your mix (and EQ, if you have it) for each one, and save. By showtime everything's locked in.

## 9. The Menu & Fader Size

Tap the ☰ icon (top right) to open the menu. It has two pages:

- **Main page** — Equalizer (Section 6, if enabled), Save Mix (Section 7), Ear Scenes (Section 8), and **Fader Layout**
- **Ear Scenes page** — tap **Ear Scenes** from the main page to get here; tap **‹** to go back

### Fader Layout

Three size options — **Compact**, **Normal**, and **Large** — change how big the faders and channel rows are. Pick whichever is easiest for your screen size and fingers; JPMixer remembers your choice on this device.

## 10. If You Lose Connection

If your phone loses WiFi or goes to sleep, JPMixer reconnects automatically as soon as the connection is restored. You don't need to refresh the page or re-select your mix — it picks up exactly where it left off.

### What the Overlays Mean

| Overlay / Indicator | What it means |
|--------------------|---------------|
| "Connecting to console…" | JPMixer is starting up, wait a few seconds |
| "Reconnecting…" | Brief WiFi drop, reconnecting automatically (appears after about 2.5 seconds so a quick blip doesn't flash the screen) |
| Connection dot not lit | No connection right now, trying to reconnect |
| Green connection dot | Live and connected |

> **If it stays on "Reconnecting…" for more than 30 seconds:** Check that your phone is still on the stage WiFi — it may have switched to mobile data. Toggle WiFi off and back on in your phone settings and it should reconnect within a few seconds.

## 11. Tips for a Better Experience

- **Always use Safari on iPhone/iPad** — Chrome and other iOS browsers don't support all the features JPMixer uses
- **Add it to your home screen** — keeps the connection alive when your screen locks, much more reliable than a browser tab
- **Stay on stage WiFi** — if your phone switches to mobile data the mixer will disconnect. Keep WiFi on and avoid areas with poor WiFi coverage
- **Don't force-close the app mid-show** — if you swipe it away on iOS, it will need to reconnect when you reopen it. Leave it running in the background instead
- **Use My Mixes for "your sound," Ear Scenes for "this song"** — My Mixes are permanent and carry across shows; Ear Scenes reset every time JPMixer launches, so they're strictly for this particular show
- **Save your Ear Scenes before the show, not during** — saving one is quick and safe to do live if you need to, but it's cleaner to dial everything in during soundcheck
- **Brightness** — turn your screen brightness up in dim stage lighting so you can read the faders clearly

## 12. Troubleshooting

### The page won't load
- Make sure you're on the stage WiFi, not mobile data
- Double-check the address your engineer gave you — it needs to be typed exactly including the port number (e.g. `:8080` at the end)
- Ask your engineer to confirm the server is running

### I moved a fader but didn't hear anything change
- Check the connection dot — if it's not green you're not connected and moves aren't being sent
- Make sure you have the right mix selected — tap the mix bar to confirm
- Your fader might be **locked** — check the banner under the song name; tap it to unlock if it says "🔒 Mix Locked"
- Ask your engineer to check the console is connected

### My faders won't move — everything says "locked"
- This is expected once you've saved an Ear Scene for the current song — it's protecting your mix from accidental bumps
- Tap the status banner under the song name to unlock and edit
- If you actually want to edit a *different* song's saved mix instead of unlocking the live one, use ☰ → Ear Scenes → Browse Ear Scenes (Section 8)

### My saved mixes from last week are gone
- This is expected if they were **Ear Scenes** — those intentionally reset every time JPMixer is relaunched, so every show starts clean
- If you wanted something permanent, save it as a **My Mix** (Section 7) instead — those never get wiped

### My Ear Scenes aren't recalling automatically
- Ask your monitor engineer to enable auto-recall in JPMixer's settings
- Make sure you saved your mix while the correct song name was showing in the snapshot bar — if it showed "—" when you saved, the save may not have attached to the right song

### The faders jumped to different positions on their own
- This is expected behaviour if a new snapshot fired and you have a saved Ear Scene for it
- If it happens unexpectedly, check with your engineer whether auto-recall is enabled

### The mixer is showing "Reconnecting…" during the show
- Check your WiFi signal — move closer to the access point if possible
- Toggle WiFi off and back on on your phone
- If you added JPMixer to your home screen, close and reopen the app — it will reconnect in seconds

### I accidentally muted everything
- If you hit **Solo** on a channel that has no signal, your mix will appear silent — tap the lit S button to release the solo
- Check for any lit **M** buttons — tap them to unmute
